# Credits and sources

- **James Pugmire**: *Every Number Is A Rule* and its companion workbooks (arithmetic, algebra, geometry), the useful pattern and its framework text, and the method this repo teaches. `book/` holds the workbooks; guides 26 and 27 quote the pattern.
- **Part V, "What The Machines Taught"**, and the microcontroller work behind guides 14 to 16 and 28: the project's chip builds (TIDE, NAGARE, FLOWING, MINAMO, CANDLE, OS 4 and others), published in https://github.com/ApplePiesFromScratch/Microcontrollers-and-robotics-beta (MIT, James Pugmire).
- **propagation-logic** (https://github.com/ApplePiesFromScratch/propagation-logic, MIT, MathofLogic): the V / G / θ framing, the six-step mapping guide, morphisms between carriers, the tier ladder with OPEN and the paid fraction. Guides 18, 19 and 21 restate them in this repo's words.
- **math-of-logic** (https://github.com/ApplePiesFromScratch/math-of-logic, MIT, Math of Logic): the kernel's `certify` (grid, gauge, falsifier), leaving V and channel preservation, the five guardrails, `=` as a scale, the closure scans, the operation count against the difference quotient, the bridge sheet and teacher notes, and the code-as-carrier notes. `ratecalc/certify.py` and `ratecalc/alphabet.py` were written for this repo after that kernel; guides 18, 19, 20 and 29 draw on its docs.
- **Engineering** (https://github.com/ApplePiesFromScratch/Engineering): *Formal Systems Engineering, a V, G, θ Workbook*. Its licence is CC BY-NC 4.0, so it is linked here, not copied.
- **Math** and **Modal-logic** (https://github.com/ApplePiesFromScratch/Math, https://github.com/ApplePiesFromScratch/Modal-logic): related studios; linked, not copied.

Other names in the guides (dual numbers, forward-mode automatic differentiation, stable fluids, Navier–Stokes, the Boussinesq approximation, Kripke frames) are cited as what a standard course calls the same or a nearby construction.
